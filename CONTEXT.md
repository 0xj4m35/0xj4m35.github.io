# CV Site

This context describes the public professional profile published as a static website.

## Language

**CV**:
The public career document that summarizes Nguyen Thai Bao's experience, projects, education, certifications, honors, and contact channels.
_Avoid_: Resume, profile

**Role**:
A position held at a company or project, including title, dates, impact, responsibilities, and technologies.
_Avoid_: Job, gig

**Work Experience**:
The CV section that presents professional roles in reverse-chronological order.
_Avoid_: Employment history, career timeline

**Impact Metric**:
A quantified outcome attached to a role that demonstrates scale, performance, or business impact.
_Avoid_: Statistic, KPI, number

**Skill Group**:
A themed collection of related skills shown together in the CV skills section.
_Avoid_: Skill category, stack group

**Project**:
A significant personal or academic product outside regular employment.
_Avoid_: Side project, portfolio item

**Theme Mode**:
The visitor-facing display preference for the CV Site, resolved from the system theme by default and manually toggled between light and dark.
_Avoid_: Color mode, appearance setting
